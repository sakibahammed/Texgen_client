## 6. About Us (about.php)

**"From Fabric to Fashion — Powered by Commitment"**

Founded in 1999 with strong entrepreneurship and skilled expertise, TEXGEN Group began as a knit fabric manufacturer to meet global market needs. Over time, it has grown into a composite and garment manufacturer, pioneering the use of European technology in knit fabric production in Bangladesh. Starting with a 3.0-ton daily capacity, Texgen now produces 16.5 tons of knit fabric, 8,500 sets of collars & cuffs, and operates two garment units with 442 machines, capable of making 15,000 T-Shirts and 8,000 Polo Shirts daily. While not the largest in capacity, Texgen's strength lies in its quality, commitment, and adaptability to ever-changing customer demands.

### Mission & Vision (as published)

To endorse our commitment to these standards, we have adopted the social accountability international standard, as the workplace code of conduct for our suppliers production facilities throughout our global network. ## 7. Factory Departments

### Knitting (`kintting.php`)
Our knitting operations are driven by customer needs, offering flexible production runs and a wide variety of fabric styles. Behind every garment lies the strength of our knitting department, where premium yarns and precisely calibrated processes create fabrics that meet exact specifications for texture, strength, and color—while upholding eco-friendly practices. Whether for large orders or specialty designs, we consistently deliver reliable quality and on-time service trusted by global brands.
*Images: kn1.jpg, kn2.jpg, kn3.jpg*

### Sample & Development (`sample.php`)
Our Sample & Development section blends eco-conscious practices with advanced technology, from color matching to texture testing. Each prototype showcases the quality and attention to detail that define TEXGEN production, enabling customers to review fit, style, and finish before placing final orders. This approach shortens lead times and ensures every production run starts with a tested, sustainable design that meets client expectations.
*Images: Sample1–3.jpg*

### Cutting (`cutting.php`)
Our cutting unit combines state-of-the-art automated machines with skilled technical expertise. At the heart of our operations, the department follows eco-friendly practices, handles fabrics with care, and ensures precise pattern alignment, transforming premium fabrics into perfectly shaped products—reflecting our commitment to timely delivery and minimal fabric waste.
*Images: cutting1–3.jpg*

### Sewing (`sewing.php`)
Our sewing unit combines skilled craftsmanship with advanced machinery to turn carefully prepared fabrics into finished products of exceptional quality. From intricate stitching to precise seam alignment, every stage is executed with attention to detail, ensuring durability, consistency, and timely delivery. Eco-conscious practices and efficient workflows further reflect our commitment to sustainable and reliable production.
*Images: Sewing1–3.jpg*

### Finishing (`finishing.php`)
Finishing is where every detail is perfected. Our team combines eco-friendly processes with advanced technology to press, trim, and refine each garment to its ideal form. Through rigorous quality checks and careful handling, we deliver products that are flawlessly finished, sustainably produced, and ready for on-time shipment.
*Images: Finishing1–3.jpg*

### Testing Lab (`testing.php`)
Our in-house testing lab ensures that every fabric and finished product meets the highest international standards. Equipped with advanced equipment, we perform a full range of tests—color fastness, shrinkage, strength, and durability—under controlled conditions. Skilled technicians monitor each stage, providing reliable data that guarantees consistent quality, regulatory compliance, and customer confidence.
*Images: TestingLab1–3.jpg*

### Store (`store.php`)
Our store department is the hub for all raw materials and finished products. From accessories and high-quality yarn to grey fabrics and completed goods, every item is carefully cataloged and stored in a climate-controlled environment. Advanced inventory systems track movement in real time, ensuring accurate stock levels, efficient retrieval, and timely delivery to production or customers.
*Images: store1–3.jpg*
