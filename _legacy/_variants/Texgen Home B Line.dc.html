We are not the largest. We are the one that adapts.

Texgen Group began as a knit fabric manufacturer serving global markets and has since grown into a composite and garment manufacturer, pioneering European knitting technology in Bangladesh.

Our strength is quality, commitment, and the ability to adapt to what each customer actually needs. Manufacturing runs through Radiance Knitwears Limited, our facility in Ashulia, Dhaka.
